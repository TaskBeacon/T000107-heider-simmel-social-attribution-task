# Assets

`animations/*.mp4` are deterministic, original geometric-motion stimuli generated by `scripts/generate_animations.py`.

- 960 × 540 pixels, 24 fps, 15 seconds, silent H.264 MP4.
- Agents are two triangles and one circle; a dark outlined enclosure is present in every clip.
- Social clips use contingent pursuit/helping trajectories.
- Mechanical clips use fixed collision or conveyor constraints.
- Random clips use independently seeded smooth waypoint trajectories with no inter-agent contingency.
- No frame, path, timing trace, or media from the 1944 film or later published stimulus sets is copied.

The generated `animations/manifest.json` records dimensions, duration, frame rate, generator version, and SHA-256 hashes.
